## Added after check run 1

The first checker run (`runs/2026-11-02-run-1-checked.md`) caught all 5 planted errors and also corrected 3 records written as accurate. Each of those 3 corrections was confirmed on the organiser's own page on 2 November 2026, so the records were wrong, not the checker. The records were fixed and the facts are added here.

- **plain 1, Infosecurity Europe.** Infosec Meets is sold to exhibitors as an add-on: "Contact our sales team today to get this game-changing networking platform added to your Infosecurity Europe exhibitor package." A vendor on a Channel Pass who is not exhibiting cannot use it to book buyer meetings. Source: https://www.infosecurityeurope.com/en-gb/visit/infosec-meets.html
- **plain 2, Accountex London.** "In Practice Visitors - 63%" (accountants, bookkeepers, practice owners) and "In Industry Visitors - 37%" (FDs, CFOs, heads of finance). The FD Show is the co-located show for senior in-house finance. Source: https://www.accountex.co.uk/london/why-exhibit/. Because visiting is for accounting and finance professionals only, a vendor's route in is exhibiting.
- **plain 5, Multimodal.** "Non-exhibiting companies attending Clarion Events are requested not to solicit for business on the show floor or from exhibitors within the show environs and may be asked to leave." Source: https://www.multimodal.org.uk/admissions-policy
