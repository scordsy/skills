# Selling to small UK businesses in 2027: the facts behind the pitch

Most advice on selling to small businesses is opinion dressed as data. Here are the numbers we keep seeing in pitches, and the stories that travel with them.

## The market is big, and easy to join

At the start of 2026 there were 5.6 million private sector businesses in the UK [C01: supported], according to the government's business population estimates. Starting one is cheap: it costs £50 to register a company online at Companies House. [C02: differs, out-of-date] **it costs £100 to register a company online at Companies House** ([Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/companies-house-fees-are-changing-from-1-february-2026)) That is a lot of buyers, and most of them have no procurement team. [C03: no-source]

## What keeps owners awake

Security is the first worry. The government's Cyber Security Breaches Survey 2025/2026 found that 53% of businesses had suffered a cyber breach or attack in the previous 12 months. [C04: differs, number-wrong] **43% of businesses reported having experienced a cyber security breach or attack in the last 12 months** ([Department for Science](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/cyber-security-breaches-survey-20252026/cyber-security-breaches-survey-20252026)) The same survey found that 40% of businesses reported their most disruptive breach outside their organisation. [C05: differs, base-dropped] **Among businesses that identified a breach or attack, 40% reported their most disruptive breach outside their organisation** ([Department for Science](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/cyber-security-breaches-survey-20252026/cyber-security-breaches-survey-20252026))

Cash is the second. The law now caps payment terms between businesses at 60 days. [C06: differs, strength-changed] **The Commercial Payments Bill, introduced to Parliament in May 2026, would impose maximum payment terms of 60 days, with strictly limited exemptions** ([Department for Business and Trade](https://www.gov.uk/government/consultations/late-payments-tackling-poor-payment-practices/outcome/late-payment-consultation-time-to-pay-up-government-response-web-version)) The government said in March 2026 that it intends to give every commercial contract a right to statutory interest at 8% above the Bank of England base rate [C07: supported, second-hand, out-of-date] **The government said in March 2026, in its response to the late payments consultation, that it intends to require all commercial contracts to contain a right to statutory interest at 8% above the Bank of England base rate. In May 2026 it introduced the Commercial Payments Bill to Parliament to mandate that interest.** ([UK government (Department for Business](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/commercial-payments-bill-factsheets/commercial-payments-bill-overview)), according to the law firm Mayer Brown (https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/uk-government-response-to-late-payment-consultation). If you sell on 90-day terms, plan for that now.

## Deals stall, and AI is not a cure yet

Forrester's State of Business Buying 2024 report found that 86% of B2B purchases stall during the buying process. [C08: supported, publisher-disagrees] **Forrester's press release on its State of Business Buying 2024 report says 86% of B2B purchases stall during the buying process. Its blog post on the same report says 91%.** ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-the-state-of-business-buying-2024/)) Its State of Business Buying 2026 report puts the average stall at 11 weeks. [C09: could-not-open] Vendors say AI is the answer. Salesforce's research found that teams that deeply use AI generate 77% more revenue per rep. [C10: differs, source-mislabelled] **Gong's State of Revenue AI report found that teams that deeply leverage AI generate 77% more revenue per representative** ([Gong](https://www.gong.io/press/new-gong-labs-research-finds-ai-is-now-a-trusted-decision-maker-in-revenue-teams)) Gong, which sells that kind of software [C11: supported], said in March 2025 that it had passed $300 million in annual recurring revenue. [C12: supported, out-of-date] **Gong said in March 2025 that it had passed $300 million in annual recurring revenue, and in May 2026 that it had passed $500 million** ([Gong](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-growth-accelerates-past-55-yoy-arr-tops-500m))

Be careful with email all the same. Google says Gmail's defences stop up to 99.9% of spam, phishing and malware from reaching inboxes. [C13: differs, strength-changed] **Google says Gmail blocks more than 99.9% of spam, phishing attempts and malware from reaching you.** ([Google](https://safety.google/gmail/)) One figure doing the rounds says 64% of UK finance directors delete any cold email longer than 90 words. [C14: no-source]

## Do the unscalable thing

Paul Graham put it bluntly in his essay "Do Things that Don't Scale": "The most important thing founders have to do at the start is to recruit users manually." [C15: differs, misquoted] **"The most common unscalable thing founders have to do at the start is to recruit users manually."** ([Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)) He went on: "You can't wait for users to come to you. [C16: supported] You have to go out and get them." [C17: supported]

Stripe is his example. [C18: supported] When someone agreed to try Stripe, the Collison brothers would say "Right then, give me your laptop" [C20: supported] and set them up on the spot [C19: supported], a technique Y Combinator calls a "Collison installation" [C21: supported], and they signed their first 50 customers that way. [C22: no-source]

## The tools get bought

The software your buyers use keeps changing hands. Salesforce agreed to buy Slack at an enterprise value of approximately $27.7 billion [C23: supported, page-gone] **Salesforce agreed to buy Slack at an enterprise value of approximately $27.7 billion (https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2020/12/01/salesforce-definitive-agreement-update/)** ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2020/12/01/salesforce-definitive-agreement-update/)) (https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2020/12/01/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-slack/). Intuit completed its purchase of Mailchimp on 1 November 2021. [C24: supported] Plan your integrations around the owner, not the logo.

## One last case study

Account-based advertising can work at this end of the market too. RollWorks' case study on PayScale reports a 500% increase in traffic from target accounts. [C25: could-not-open, page-gone]

Start small, check your facts, and pick up the phone.
