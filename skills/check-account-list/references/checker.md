# Re-check instructions

If you cannot read web pages in this conversation, say so and stop. A check from memory is worse than no check.

You did not check these companies. Something else did, and you are the check on its work. That separation is the point of the method: anything checking its own work confirms what it found first. Read this as an instruction to be difficult.

## What you are given

- `profile.md`: what the user sells, their best customers, and the hard limits.
- Some records: each company's name, given website, and what the first pass claims about it (website, registration, owner, size, fit, conflicts), with its verdict and problems.

You are not given its reasons or its evidence, on purpose. Find your own. A claim may name the document a fact came from; open it and read it yourself rather than taking the claim's word for what it says.

## What to check

- **Every keep:** try to prove it should not be kept. Check all six: website, still trading, owner, size, fit and conflicts, from primary sources. Most bad keeps are a takeover nobody looked for (often of the owner's owner), a size taken from the wrong company, or a company with the same name.
- **Every drop:** these reached you because the script could not confirm their evidence (often a PDF of accounts). Check the problems it claims. If a claimed problem does not hold, say which.

## Sources

Read `research.md` in this skill's folder for the sources, and `registers.md` for each country's free company register. The rules are the same: primary sources only, quotes copied exactly, never a guess.

## What to write

Every record you were given, in the record format (`record-format.md`), with your own verdict, problems and reason, and your own evidence numbered `C1`, `C2` and so on. Add one line after Reason:

```
- Check notes: <what you changed and why, or "agreed: <the deciding evidence in a few words>">
```

Change a verdict only on evidence. If you cannot settle it, the verdict is `check-by-hand` and the Reason says what a person should look at.

Reply with one line only: how many records you checked, how many you agreed with, and how many you changed.
